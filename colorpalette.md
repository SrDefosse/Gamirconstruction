# Gamir Construction Color Palette

This palette is defined in `app/app.css` and is the visual foundation for the site.

| Role | Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Ink | `--ink` | `#18211D` | Primary typography, dark surfaces, primary buttons, and the preloader. |
| Warm Paper | `--paper` | `#F4F1EB` | Main page surface, light buttons, and light navigation states. |
| Architectural Sand | `--sand` | `#E5DFD5` | Secondary section backgrounds and form surfaces. |
| Hill Country Moss | `--moss` | `#65725A` | Accent surfaces, kicker labels, and final calls to action. |
| Structural Line | `--line` | `rgba(24, 33, 29, .18)` | Dividers, input borders, and quiet visual structure. |

## Usage principles

- Keep Deep Ink and Warm Paper as the primary contrast pair.
- Use Hill Country Moss as the single accent, chiefly for meaningful actions and section transitions.
- Architectural Sand should support content, never compete with photography.
- On a light navigation surface, use Deep Ink for links and the logo. On the dark hero, invert the logo and use Warm Paper for links.
